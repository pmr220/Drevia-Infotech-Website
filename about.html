How we work, from first call to after-service.

Ten steps, in this order, every time.

01
🧠

We understand the pain points of your business

Before any screen or code, we sit with you and learn what slows you down, what costs you money and what your customers complain about.

02
🔍

We analyse

We study your workflow, your users and the tools you use today to find the real problem under the visible one.

03
🗺️

We make a plan

A clear plan with scope, screens, features and timeline, written in plain language you can read in one sitting.

04
💬

We discuss it with you

We walk you through the plan and change it until it feels right to you. Nothing is built before you agree.

05
➕

We take additional requirements

Ideas come after you see the plan. We collect every extra requirement now, so it is designed in rather than patched on.

06
⚙️

We make it

Our team builds the product with clean, maintainable code, the way it was planned.

07
👀

We take reviews while making

You see work in progress and give feedback during the build, not after it. Course corrections are cheap early.

08
🧪

We test it in 3 phases

Phase one tests each feature by itself. Phase two tests the whole product working together. Phase three is a real-world trial before launch.
