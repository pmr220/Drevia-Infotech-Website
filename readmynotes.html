Questions people ask.

Who can upload material?

Teachers who register with a teacher profile. An admin can review uploads before they go live.

How does a student get only their own university's material?

Students choose a university and stream when they sign up. Every search and every alert is limited to that choice, and they can switch whenever they want.

Can we add our own university?

Yes. Universities, streams, semesters and subjects are set up by the admin, so the library matches your region.

Is it free for students?

Pricing is decided with you when we build it. The app is designed so students can use it without friction.

How is it different from DigiClasss?

DigiClasss is a private management system for one institute. ReadMyNotes is an open library for students of a university and stream.
